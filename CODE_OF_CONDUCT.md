# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are trying to help. Criticise the code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Disagree about the work, not about each other. "This will break when the
  socket is stale" is useful; "you clearly did not think" is not.
- Take the report seriously before deciding it is wrong. Most bug reports that
  sound impossible turn out to be true.
- Say what you actually verified, and what you did not. Guesses presented as
  findings waste more time than silence.
- Remember that not everybody is writing in their first language.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Sexual language or imagery.
Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of an issue or a
pull request.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/ryanbekhen/dermaga/security/advisories/new)
— it is the private channel this project has, and it is not only for security.

Reports are handled by the maintainer. What happens next depends on what
happened: usually a word in private, sometimes an edit or a locked thread, and
for anything sustained or deliberate, a ban.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this
project.
